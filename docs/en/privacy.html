Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: Smile shows no ads, sells no data and doesn't analyse you for advertising. Your photos and videos are seen only by the people in the albums you send them to, and by the Frames that show those albums.

1. Who is responsible

Skygraphy Copter Technologies e.U., owner DI Dr. Ernst Schiener, Waldgasse 13, 3400 Klosterneuburg, Austria. Email: admin@skygraphy.com. Write to this address with any question about privacy.

2. What data Smile processes

Your account

  • Email address: to sign in with a code sent by email. There is no password.
  • Name and, if you like, a profile picture: so others see who sent a photo.
  • If you like, a Smile name (@name): so others can invite you. It's only found when typed exactly.

What you share

  • Photos and videos, their captions and the date they were taken. Smile reads that date from the file before it's shrunk; it's used for the “On this day” feature.
  • Who sent what and when, hearts and photos kept on a Frame, hidden photos and the trash.
  • Your Spaces, albums and roles (member, co-admin, admin, Frame admin), invitations and requests to join.
  • The language of your app, so notifications reach you in that language.

Frames

  • For each Frame: name, device model, app version, battery level, whether it's charging, when it was last online, its settings and, if set, an unreadable form of the child lock code.
  • A Frame has no account of its own. It's paired with a Space by QR code and only receives the albums assigned to it.

Notifications

  • So Smile can tell you about new photos, invitations and requests and update Frames right away, it uses Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. Each device gets a random identifier (token) for this, which Smile stores. You turn notifications off in Android's settings.

Inviting from contacts

  • Only if you use this feature and allow Smile access. Your contacts' email addresses are made unreadable on your phone (SHA-256) and only sent in that form to check who already uses Smile. Smile doesn't store them. Names and phone numbers never leave your phone. You send an invitation by text message or WhatsApp yourself, with that app.

Subscriptions

  • Frame subscriptions are bought and paid for through Google Play; Smile never sees your payment details. Managing the subscriptions (whether a purchase is genuine, renewal, cancellation, trial) is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier of your Space and the purchase data from Google Play for this, but neither your name nor your email address, photos or other content.

Crash reports

  • If Smile crashes, it sends a report through Google's Firebase Crashlytics: device type, Android version, app version and where in the program the error happened. Photos, videos and content are never included.

Technical data

  • Like any server, our services receive your device's IP address with each connection. It's only kept in the providers' short-lived technical logs and isn't used to identify you.
  • Settings such as sounds, video quality or tips, and photos still waiting to be sent, stay on your phone.

3. Who sees your photos

Smile isn't public. A photo is seen by the members of the album it was sent to, by the members of other Spaces this album is explicitly shared with, and by the Frames the album is assigned to. A Frame admin manages a Frame and sees what that Frame shows. A Space's admins see its albums, Frames and storage use.

5. Legal bases

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, albums, photos, Frames, notifications and subscriptions are the service you use with Smile.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): access to contacts and permission for notifications. You can withdraw both at any time in Android's settings.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): crash reports and technical logs, so Smile runs safely and without errors.

6. How long data is kept

  • Your data is kept as long as you have your account.
  • A deleted photo, album or Space first goes to the trash and is deleted for good after 30 days, files included.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, all your photos and videos in all albums and all your memberships are deleted right away. Spaces where you're the admin go to your longest-standing co-admin; without a co-admin, the Space is deleted with all its albums and Frames. How to delete your account.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Google after 90 days.
  • Google Play and RevenueCat keep subscription purchase data as long as statutory retention duties for accounting and tax require.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to admin@skygraphy.com. You can save photos and videos to your phone in the app at any time. If you think we don't process your data correctly, you can complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).

8. Children

Smile is meant for adults. Children can watch a Frame that adults set up for them and whose albums they choose; children don't need an account for this.

9. This website

smileapp.app sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services and only loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives your device's IP address when doing so. The pages for invite links ask our server whether an invitation is still valid.
