Delete your Smile account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time. It happens right away and can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Smile and tap ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the very bottom, type your account's email address to confirm and tap Delete.

Without the app

If you no longer have Smile on your phone, write from your account's email address to admin@skygraphy.com with the subject “Delete account”. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm it to you by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile with name, profile picture and Smile name.
  • All photos and videos you sent, in all albums, with captions, hearts and files.
  • All your memberships, roles, invitations and requests, and the identifiers for notifications.
  • Spaces where you're the admin: your longest-standing co-admin takes them over. If there's no co-admin, the Space is deleted with all its albums, photos and Frames.

Nothing is left behind. The providers' technical logs, which only contain IP addresses, disappear by themselves after a short time. More in the privacy policy.
